**Mission 9 — Build your own AI-use rule**
Objective: Create five rules you will follow when using AI for engineering learning.
My five-rule AI working agreement

Rule 1 — Verify important claims
I will check important technical answers using official documentation, reliable references or experiments before relying on them.

Rule 2 — Protect confidential information
I will not upload confidential company data, proprietary chip designs, passwords or restricted engineering documents to an AI service without explicit authorisation.

Rule 3 — Understand before using
I will read AI-generated code and explanations and make sure I can explain how they work.

Rule 4 — Test the result
I will run small experiments, test code and examine the results wherever practical instead of trusting an answer just because it sounds confident.

Rule 5 — Take responsibility
I will take responsibility for my submitted work and engineering decisions, acknowledge AI assistance when required, and correct mistakes I discover.

Justification
During Mission 4, I learned that an LLM generates answers based on learned patterns and context, and that its explanations may need clarification. During Mission 5, I used a Python program and official documentation to verify a factual claim. These activities show why I should check important AI-generated answers instead of accepting them without evidence.
