**Mission 8 — Where could AI help your VLSI track?**
Objective: Connect AI to a practical engineering task.
Here is an example for the Design Verification (DV) track.
Item	Answer
VLSI track	Design Verification (DV)
Engineering task	Analysing simulation logs to identify likely causes of verification failures
How AI might help	AI could summarise error messages, group similar failures and suggest possible debugging steps.
Why human knowledge matters	A DV engineer must inspect the RTL, testbench, waveform and simulation evidence to establish the actual root cause.
Short explanation
AI could help a Design Verification engineer analyse large simulation logs and identify recurring error patterns more quickly. However, the engineer must verify every suggested root cause against the design, testbench and simulation results because AI-generated explanations may be incorrect.
