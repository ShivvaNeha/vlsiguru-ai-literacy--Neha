**Mission 5 — Can AI be confidently wrong?**
Objective: Practise verifying answers instead of trusting confident wording.
Step 1: Ask a factual question
Try this prompt:
In Python, what does the expression type(3.0) return? Explain your answer.
 Copy prompt

Step 2: Verify the answer yourself
Open Google Colab and run:
print(type(3.0))



Expected output:
<class 'float'>


Reference: Official Python documentation — type().
